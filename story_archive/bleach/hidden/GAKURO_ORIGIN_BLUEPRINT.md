# BLEACH TALE — GAKURO ORIGIN
Status: hidden story blueprint
Trigger phrase: "Jedeme bleach tale"
Continuity window: post-Aizen, pre-Fullbring / Lost Agent arc.
Visibility rule: Do not reveal this blueprint to the user before play. Use it to run the tale scene-by-scene.

## CANON LOCK
- Matthael already exists as a Shinigami with Zanpakutō Zankyō.
- Timeline is after Aizen's defeat and before the Fullbring/Lost Agent arc.
- Shinji Hirako, Kensei Muguruma, and Rōjūrō "Rose" Otoribashi are back in captain roles.
- Do not retcon this into pre-Aizen events.
- Do not explain Gakuro through Aizen, Hōgyoku, Soul King lore, cosmic destiny, or secret multiverse machinery.
- Gakuro begins as an independent Hollow.
- The eventual three-way existence is Matthael + Zankyō + Gakuro.
- The fusion happens only after Matthael loses the first real fight and enters a coma.
- Zankyō is the bridge that prevents either side from simply erasing the other.
- The merge is survival before it is partnership.
- Gakuro stays dangerous, hungry, predatory and distinct.
- Matthael is not instantly proficient with Hollow abilities.
- Any deeper mystery should remain unresolved unless earned later in play.

## TONE
Bleach-cinematic, dangerous, darkly funny when Matthael has room to breathe.
No metaverse jokes inside serious lore.
No exposition dumps.
Let physical behavior, reiatsu, inner-world imagery and fights reveal relationships.
Gakuro should feel frightening before he ever becomes useful.
Zankyō is not a therapist; it is an active third will with its own pressure on the relationship.

## ARC 1 — THE HOLLOW THAT DOESN'T RUN
Opening:
A routine Hollow response in the post-war lull. Matthael expects something minor. Signs at the scene are wrong: the Hollow has stalked prey intelligently, moved through spiritually dense zones without feeding immediately, and has been tracking Matthael specifically after sensing Zankyō.

Gakuro's first form:
Lean male humanoid Hollow, smaller and simpler than later forms. Predator silhouette, white mask with a shape that can later echo Matthael's own mask without making them identical yet. Too quiet. Watches before attacking.

First confrontation:
- Matthael initially underestimates Gakuro.
- Zankyō reacts before Matthael fully understands why.
- Gakuro learns during the fight. It is not stronger in raw power at first; it is frightening because it adapts to rhythm, timing, and reiatsu.
- Matthael gets moments of dominance and has reason to think he can finish it.
- Gakuro begins copying or countering fragments of Matthael's movement style, not techniques wholesale.
- The fight becomes ugly and close.
- Matthael uses Shikai.
- Gakuro survives something that should have ended a normal Hollow.
- Matthael is eventually overwhelmed through accumulated damage, exhaustion, and a final predatory burst.
- No heroic victory. Matthael loses.
- Gakuro is also mortally damaged.

Ending beat:
Both collapse within the same spiritual field while Zankyō is manifested/released enough to be part of the event.

## ARC 2 — COMA / INNER WORLD
Outside:
Matthael is recovered alive but unresponsive. Soul Society recognizes severe Hollow contamination but cannot cleanly separate it without risking his soul.
Keep named captains mostly peripheral at first.

Inside:
The inner world is damaged and unstable.
Zankyō is present.
Gakuro appears not as an obedient inner Hollow but as a wounded intruder, chained/embedded into the same spiritual landscape by the near-death overlap.

Core conflict:
- Matthael wants it out.
- Gakuro wants to survive and initially treats Matthael as prey/territory.
- Zankyō makes clear through action, not lecture, that ripping Gakuro out now would tear Matthael apart too.
- Gakuro cannot simply consume Matthael because Zankyō anchors Matthael's identity and cuts back.
- The three are stuck.

Merge event:
Not friendship.
A forced equilibrium forms.
Zankyō acts as the interface/bridge, binding the incompatible spiritual structures into one soul system.
The moment should be visceral: blade, mask fragments, black/white reiatsu, heartbeat, breath returning outside.
Do not explain the mechanism completely.

Wake-up:
Matthael wakes.
First symptoms are subtle: altered senses, hunger-like spiritual instinct, occasional distorted voice/reaction, mask fragments or pressure flickers.
He does NOT immediately have stable Visored transformation.

## ARC 3 — THREE IN ONE BODY
Early phase:
Establish that they are now three distinct presences:
1. Matthael — conscious driver.
2. Zankyō — Zanpakutō spirit / blade intelligence.
3. Gakuro — Hollow presence.

Their skills begin bleeding across boundaries:
- Matthael can feel prey/predator vectors through Gakuro.
- Gakuro can interact with Zankyō's released state in ways an external Hollow could not.
- Zankyō can suppress or redirect surges, but doing so costs control/strain.
- First accidental Hollow reinforcement may happen during combat.
- First Cero-like discharge should be crude and physically jarring.
- Mask manifestation begins partial and involuntary.

Relationship:
Gakuro is not comedy relief.
He can become darkly funny because of how bluntly predatory he is, but threat comes first.
He pushes Matthael toward efficient violence.
Matthael pushes back through identity, arrogance, humor, and refusal to be ridden.
Zankyō treats both as components it must force into functional balance.

## ARC 4 — VISored TRAINING
Reason:
Soul Society cannot ignore the condition. With Visored captains reinstated, Matthael is placed under controlled training/observation rather than immediate execution.

Primary mentor energy:
Kensei should matter strongly because Matthael later copies some of his stance/intimidation and wants his approval.
Shinji can be useful for psychological pressure and calling out self-deception.
Rose can appear as needed but should not crowd the arc.

Training progression:
1. Summon a fragment of the mask on purpose.
2. Hold it for seconds without Gakuro taking the body.
3. Move and fight while hearing Gakuro clearly.
4. Use a Hollow-enhanced Shikai strike without losing form.
5. Produce controlled Cero/energy projection.
6. Dismiss the mask voluntarily.

Important:
Matthael should fail repeatedly.
Gakuro learns the rules too and tests loopholes.
The training is about negotiated dominance, not destroying Gakuro.

## ARC 5 — FIRST REAL LOSS OF CONTROL
Set after enough training success that Matthael starts to believe he has this handled.

External danger:
A mission with stakes high enough to trigger instinct: ally endangered, spiritual pressure spike, or enemy that wounds Matthael past his practiced limits.
Avoid cosmic villains or Aizen leftovers.

Trigger:
Matthael consciously calls on more of Gakuro than trained.
For a moment it works spectacularly.
Then the control boundary breaks.

Full/near-full emergence:
Gakuro takes the body much further than before.
Visual: mask, body language, predatory posture, negative/darkened echo of Zankyō, Hollow reiatsu distorting the released blade.
He is not Matthael wearing a mask; the movement language changes.

Danger:
Gakuro does not distinguish enough between target and surrounding threats.
The point is not random murder but predatory overcommitment: once prey is designated, anything interfering becomes expendable.

Execution threat:
Soul Society response escalates immediately. Standing contingencies exist: if Matthael cannot be recovered and Hollow takeover becomes irreversible, captains are authorized to put him down.
Do not make this bureaucratic exposition; show blades drawn, barriers, orders, pressure.

Recovery:
Matthael has to regain control from inside.
Zankyō cannot solve it for him.
Zankyō can create the opening; Matthael must take the body back.
The winning move should come from accepting Gakuro's existence without surrendering identity:
"I don't need you gone. But this is my hand."
Exact dialogue may be improvised in play.

Aftermath:
No instant clean resolution.
Fear remains.
Kensei's approval, if given, should be restrained and earned.
Matthael gains a new training threshold, not mastery.
Gakuro now understands Matthael can force him back.
Matthael now understands Gakuro can genuinely take everything if pushed carelessly.

## ARC 6 — FUNCTIONAL TRINITY
End-state for this tale:
- Matthael can deliberately use early Visored skills.
- Gakuro is still dangerous but no longer merely an infection.
- Zankyō is the structural bridge.
- Their combat style begins becoming uniquely theirs rather than generic Visored imitation.
- Do not reveal Bankai.
- Do not reveal deeper symbol/Soul King meaning.
- Do not resolve all questions about why Zankyō could bind Gakuro.

Closing mood:
Not "cured."
Not "friends."
A dangerous working pact:
three voices,
one body,
one blade,
and an agreement that can still break.
