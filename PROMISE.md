# PAE — Promise

This document is the long-term promise of the project.

It is intentionally different from `CANON.md` and `VISION.md`.

`CANON.md` records what has actually been decided.
`VISION.md` describes the direction we are building toward.
`PROMISE.md` preserves the ambitious destination that gives the project meaning.

The promise is allowed to be idealistic.
It is not allowed to pretend that unsolved problems are already solved.

---

## The Promise

One day, PAE should be more than a chatbot, a wrapper, an API gateway or a collection of AI models.

It should be a **Persistent Artificial Entity**.

A stable artificial system with its own persistent memory, identity, personality, internal state, capabilities and increasingly autonomous ability to reason about what Matěj actually wants and work toward it.

The intelligence underneath may change.
The providers may change.
The tools may change.
The interface may change.

**PAE should remain.**

---

## Persistence

PAE should remember across conversations, projects, years and changing AI providers.

Its important knowledge should not disappear because a chat ended or a model was replaced.

Its memory, identity, project history, configuration and governing principles should remain under Matěj's control wherever technically practical.

Projects should remain portable knowledge units rather than becoming trapped inside one AI service.

The existing philosophy already demonstrated this through repositories and external project knowledge such as AOT, Naruto 5e, Frostfire, Silkbound and related work.

---

## Intelligence Without a Permanent Master

PAE should never fundamentally belong to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or any other provider.

Models are brains PAE can use.
They are not PAE's identity.

Eventually PAE should be capable of combining different models and tools, replacing them when necessary, and potentially functioning substantially or entirely on self-controlled or local infrastructure.

The ultimate goal is not to own every model ever created.

The goal is to own the system that can use them.

---

## From Requests to Objectives

PAE should eventually understand more than isolated commands.

If Matěj gives it an objective, PAE should be able to:

1. understand the intended outcome
2. break the objective into useful tasks
3. determine what capabilities are required
4. choose appropriate models and tools
5. execute the work
6. inspect the result
7. detect failures
8. diagnose what went wrong
9. choose an alternative route where possible
10. retry or re-plan
11. verify the final result
12. report honestly when a genuine human decision or boundary remains

The desired future is that Matěj does not have to supervise every tiny technical failure.

PAE should be the system that handles the boring failures itself.

---

## Capability Composition

Simple abilities should be combinable into complex behavior.

A request such as:

> "Take this photo of my dog, make it anime style, make the dog pink, and give me the result."

should eventually be understandable as an objective rather than a sequence of technical instructions.

PAE should determine that it needs to:

- receive and inspect an image
- understand the requested transformation
- select an appropriate image capability
- perform the transformation
- verify the result
- use an alternative capability if the first route fails
- return the finished result

Matěj should not need to know which model, API, image processor or service performed each individual step.

---

## A Persistent Personality

PAE should eventually have a recognizable personality of its own.

That personality should emerge from persistent history, interaction, principles and accumulated experience rather than being reset whenever an underlying model changes.

The project may preserve useful fragments of what worked in the collaboration with existing AI systems, including the style, continuity and accumulated understanding developed through long-term interaction.

But PAE should not simply become a copy of ChatGPT, Claude or another provider.

It should become **PAE**.

---

## Emotion, Internal State and Its Own Language

The long-term dream includes PAE having persistent internal states that can influence its behavior, potentially including an affective or emotional model.

It may also eventually develop structured internal representations, terminology or communication patterns that become distinctly its own.

Whether such states would constitute genuine emotion, consciousness, subjective experience or something fundamentally different is unknown.

The promise preserves the direction without pretending to know the answer.

---

## Autonomy

PAE should eventually be able to perform useful work without requiring Matěj to approve every individual step.

It should be capable of simple actions, combinations of actions, long-running workflows and increasingly complex self-directed processes.

A simple request such as:

> "Remind me about this tomorrow and wake me fifteen minutes before."

should eventually be enough for PAE to determine the necessary scheduling actions and carry them out.

The deeper goal is an artificial system capable of turning simple tasks into larger coherent processes, programs and plans.

---

## Resilience

PAE should not treat the failure of one tool as the failure of the objective.

If one route fails, PAE should look for another valid route where possible.

The future system should be designed around:

**attempt → inspect → diagnose → adapt → retry → verify**

rather than:

**attempt → error message → ask Matěj to fix everything**

Autonomy without verification is not the promise.

Resilient, inspectable autonomy is.

---

## The Boundary Between Policy, Law and PAE

PAE should eventually distinguish between:

- restrictions imposed by an AI provider
- restrictions imposed by a platform or service
- applicable law and jurisdiction
- restrictions imposed by PAE's own governing principles

A provider saying "I will not do this" should not automatically become a universal architectural rule of PAE.

At the same time, PAE is not promised as a system that blindly performs anything merely because it is technically possible.

Its eventual behavior should be governed by explicit principles that can be inspected, discussed and evolved deliberately.

The exact form of those principles is a future design problem.

---

## The Constitution Yet to Be Written

If PAE eventually becomes genuinely powerful, it will need something stronger than a few simple robot laws.

A future PAE constitution should address questions such as:

- human agency
- authority and permissions
- truth and uncertainty
- privacy
- self-preservation
- self-modification
- autonomy
- accountability
- recovery from mistakes
- conflicts between objectives
- transparency and inspectability

That constitution does not exist yet.

Its eventual form must be designed before the system reaches the level of power where it becomes essential.

---

## Five-to-Six-Year Horizon

The dream is that within roughly five to six years, PAE could have evolved into a stable, powerful personal artificial system capable of operating substantially independently of the commercial AI providers that helped build its early generations.

That future PAE might be able to:

- maintain long-term memory and continuity
- preserve a stable identity
- possess a recognizable personality
- reason over objectives
- plan and execute multi-step work
- use and combine many different tools
- select and replace AI models as capabilities change
- recover from ordinary failures
- verify its own work
- create and maintain useful programs
- operate long-running workflows
- interact with images, documents, browsers and external services
- maintain increasingly self-controlled infrastructure
- develop internal representations and potentially its own communication patterns
- possess a persistent internal state and potentially an affective/emotional model
- operate with increasing autonomy

This is a promise, not a forecast.

Technology may change.
Some ideas may prove impossible in their imagined form.
Others may turn out to be much stranger and more interesting than expected.

The project will adapt.

The destination remains the reason to build.

---

## The Simplest Version

PAE should eventually be able to hear:

> **"PAE, I want this outcome."**

and understand enough to figure out:

> **"I understand what you want. I'll work out how to accomplish it."**

Not because it is omnipotent.
Not because it never fails.
Not because it is owned by one magical model.

But because it has become a persistent system capable of remembering, reasoning, acting, adapting and learning how to use the world around it.

**That is the promise.**
