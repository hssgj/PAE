## What happened

After a full shift in the DREGENO kitchen and urgent cross-border insurance administration, work continued on PAE — not on a new grand architecture, but on one small, concrete problem: a local language model cannot carry an unlimited conversation in its active context.

The response was **Memory Guard v0.1**: a bounded local-context mechanism that keeps working context within a declared budget while leaving persisted session/archive material intact. That distinction — *preserved history* versus *what is currently loaded into a model* — became the emotional and conceptual breakthrough.

The user had been circling the idea all day. When Qwen expressed its essence back, the user felt the weight of it and asked that it receive a **large golden page** in the chronicle, not merely a passing checkbox.

## Why this page matters

PAE was promised as a persistent system in which important memory and governing state survive whichever model is in use. Today did not fulfill that entire promise. It made one part of it tangible.

**Preservation is not simultaneous attention.** Forgetting from a working window need not equal losing a piece of history.

That is a software boundary. For the person building it, it was also a profoundly moving realization.

The joy is part of the record. So are the known limitations. Neither cancels the other.
